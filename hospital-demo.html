FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can patients book appointments online?

Yes. The customer website is designed for online appointment booking and can be connected to the hospital's NR HMS appointment dashboard.

Can the hospital add its own doctors and departments?

Yes. In the full NR HMS system, hospital admin can manage departments, specialities and doctors. The public website can display those records automatically.

Can mobile OTP verification be added?

Yes. The production appointment workflow can use mobile OTP before final confirmation.

Will the website work on mobile?

Yes. This demo is responsive and designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
