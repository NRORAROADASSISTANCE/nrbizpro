CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY

NR BizPro Refund Policy

Registration fee

The ₹2,500 registration fee is a one-time account setup charge and is generally non-refundable after account setup or activation.

Plan cancellation

Customers may request cancellation through support. Cancellation does not automatically create a refund.

Eligible refunds

If a payment was duplicated, charged incorrectly, or a technical payment issue resulted in a successful charge without the corresponding service activation, contact support with the transaction reference. Eligible refunds are reviewed and processed through the original payment method where possible.

Non-eligible situations

Refunds are generally not provided for change of mind, unused time after activation, or failure to use the software where the service was made available, subject to applicable law.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are initiated after verification. The time taken to reflect the amount depends on the payment provider and bank.

Contact

For cancellation or refund requests, use our Contact Us page and provide your registered details and payment reference.
